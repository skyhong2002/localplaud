Add audio

A new recording reaches localplaud in four steps. You never need to press Plaud's Generate button.

  1. Record with your Plaud device.
  2. Sync it to the official Plaud app so the raw audio uploads.
  3. Discover — localplaud downloads the raw audio; any existing Plaud transcript or meeting note is mirrored as a labelled reference.
  4. Generate locally — transcription, speakers, notes and Ask are created by your configured localplaud pipeline.
New files appear in All files and the recent-file sidebar.
