# Code of Conduct

localplaud welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This applies
to issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Be a considerate collaborator

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully. Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Make room for questions, mistakes, different perspectives, and accessibility
  needs. Assume good intent while taking the impact of your words seriously.
- Respect privacy. Do not post someone else's recordings, transcripts, contact
  details, credentials, or other personal information without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized remarks, personal attacks,
doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainer, [@skyhong2002](https://github.com/skyhong2002),
using GitHub's comment reporting tools or the relevant project conversation.
For concerns involving private information, use GitHub's private reporting tools
instead of posting that information publicly. Security reports belong in the
[private vulnerability channel](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, close
disruptive conversations, or restrict participation. Decisions consider context,
severity, and repeated behavior. Participants are expected to respect moderation
decisions and help keep the project welcoming and useful.
